# Notes variant — One-on-One

Use this body order (instead of the standard Phase-3 template) when Step 0 selected the
**one-on-one** notes variant — a mentor/manager ↔ report/peer 1-on-1: coaching, checking
in on someone's work, unblocking them, tactical working session. This is **person-level**
(one person's work, growth, blockers), as opposed to the **leadership** variant which is
**org-level** (execs deciding how to run the org).

Everything else in the skill (frontmatter, `## Source & Metadata`, attendee enrichment,
dual-brain mode, output variant, etc.) is unchanged; only the body sections differ.

Same rules as the other formats: **optional sections are omitted entirely when empty**,
and `Action Items` still scans for promises the user made to others (tag each `(promise)`).

## Body order

```markdown
# {Meeting Title} — {Date}

**Attendees:** {links + emails}
**Date:** {…} · **Format:** {…} · {~duration}

## Source & Metadata
{the standard metadata block — see Phase 3}

## Executive Summary
{tight skim: who/what the 1-on-1 was, the relationship/role context, what changed}

## Coaching & Guidance
{the teaching/mentoring content — concepts explained, advice given, how-to / frameworks
transferred. The knowledge that passed from one person to the other. Omit if none.}

## Their Focus & Progress
{what the person owns and is working on, progress since last time, KRs/projects/tasks
they're driving. The "where are they at" snapshot.}

## Concerns & Blockers
{what they raised as stuck/worried/unsure — the heart of a real 1-on-1. What's slowing
them down, what they need help with, what they're uncertain about. Never omit a real one.}

## Decisions & Agreements
{concrete things agreed in the call — usually lighter/more tactical than exec decisions.}

## Action Items
{concrete owner-tagged tasks; include `(promise)`-tagged commitments the user made to the
other person.}

## Next Steps
{directional moves that matter but aren't yet atomic tasks.}

## Coaching Notes / Development
{optional — observations on the person's growth, strengths, areas to develop, feedback
given or worth giving next time. The "manage the person, not just the task" layer.}

## Open Questions / Follow-ups
{optional — unresolved items to revisit at the next 1-on-1.}

## Meeting Historical Breakdown
{chronological segments with bullets — same as standard.}

<!-- timeline -->

## Timeline
- {YYYY-MM-DD} — {one-line dated summary}

## See Also
- {attendees, companies, programs; companion brain page if dual; raw transcript}
```

## Notes
- Prioritize **Concerns & Blockers** and **Their Focus & Progress** — surfacing and
  unblocking the person's work is why the 1-on-1 happened. If the call was mostly the
  senior person teaching, **Coaching & Guidance** carries the weight.
- Keep **Coaching Notes / Development** honest and specific — it's the layer that makes a
  1-on-1 page worth rereading before the next one. Omit it rather than padding.
